\begin{abstract}
Parking tickets only exist where an officer was, and in our Bengaluru data (151 days, 298{,}445 tickets, 54 police stations) officers almost vanish in the evening: 1{,}773 distinct devices are active at 11:00 but only 12 at 19:00. We ask three questions with classical machine learning only.
(1)~Is the evening gap real? (2)~On a benchmark where the truth is known by construction, how much does censoring hurt, and how well does inverse-propensity weighting recover the truth as exposure becomes small? (3)~Which classical models forecast next-day station counts and next-week hotspots best, and are their uncertainty intervals honest?
